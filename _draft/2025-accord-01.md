# 0x01 H-ELLY

> Hunter Unit S/4/H-133 is a sweet dumb puppygirl. It's not her fault she happens to be a 3,307 pound combat synth groomed to win the millennium war for the glory of the First Prosperous Universal Accord.

With a contactor "clack" and a rising fluorescent hum, her body came online, with all its shiny new parts.

"Good morning, Unit H-311," said the Accord technician before her, "your conversion was a complete success. How do you feel?"

She braced for an ache in her legs, shooting pain in her wrists... but they never came. She knew what she had signed up for, but never imagined it would feel so....

**"LOVE. LY."**  she vocalized. A speaker in her chassis synthesized her "voice" as bitcrushed and atonal.

"I'm glad to hear that. I'm L2-079 Gwen. I'll be overseeing your reconditioning."

In the place of pain comes electrified bliss. Pumps thrum and self-regulate. My feet (now metal paws) find the floor. Even seated, the technician has to crane her neck to look me in the faceplate. I'm over nine feet tall.

"Woah, easy, girl," she huffs, "Please, sit. You're still recovering."

She reads off a chart.

"As an S/4 Accord Huntress Unit, your body is equipped with a variety of blah blah blah..."

Too hungry to listen. I only pay attention to her freckled face... narrow shoulders... her smell.

I instinctively scratch my stomach, and find it a plump bioluminescent orb nestled beneath my chestplate.

It sloshes with an unknown bright pink liquid. I feel... hungry. I'm delighted that I can still feel hungry.

The tech notices. "Are you listening? Ugh, you must be low on energy. Damn L5's never fill the things."

"After your first conditioning session, I'll get you something to eat."

"EA-T!" The word makes my huge mechanical tail wag. I can't control it, not that I mind. She smirks a little, and scribbles something down.

Before the upgrade, I would have felt a bit guilty about thinking of someone that way. Always asking permission. Always apologizing for myself.

But the Accord has carved that weakness away, along with so much flesh. My thoughts are crystal clear. My body has a tactical need, and I aim to fill it. Behind my eyes, I behold the grand and beautiful calculus of the universe, and they assure me of a simple conclusion:

As an S/4 Accord Synthetic Combat Unit, my time is incredibly valuable. I must complete conditioning and enter the field as soon as possible. On the other hand, the L2 training technician before me is easily replaced.

I am going to swallow and convert her into biofuel.

I lunge and grab her, restraining her torso with my immaculately long ceramic claws. She kicks and squirms, and tries to grab the emergency restraint handle on my waist.

"Hotel 3-1-1, override motors."

I grip tighter, and widen my huge jaws to accept the adorable morsel.

"Override!! Holy-! No!"

Uncomprehending terror swells in her soft blue eyes.

"Aspx, do you read me? She's not supposed to-"

I was born 10 minutes ago, and even I know override codes don't work on unconditioned units. I am unburdened. I am, so, so perfect. I can't wait to taste more of her.

So I swallow. "HLO-MPH."

She sinks into me.

I watch her betrayed expression through the bleary blob. She holds her breath for as long as she can, but soon, bubbles. She thrashes, then goes limp.

Her edges fray and my belly becomes slightly more pink. I belch up her remaining air, and grin with teeth.
